The Epic

The Story That Lives in Every Heart

From the royal palace of Ayodhya to the forests of Dandakaranya, from the golden deer to the burning of Lanka — Ramleela retells the journey of Shri Ram, Sita Mata, Lakshman and Hanuman in a spectacular live performance.

Our troupe brings centuries-old tradition together with modern stagecraft: dramatic lighting, hand-crafted costumes, classical choreography and an original live score.

The Performance

A Complete Spectacle

❖

Three art forms, one unforgettable evening.

🎭

Acting

Powerful dialogue and expressive characters — from Ram's calm resolve to Ravana's thunderous ego — performed by seasoned stage actors.

💃

Dance

Classical and folk choreography: the Swayamvar, the Vanvaas, Hanuman's leap across the ocean and the grand victory celebration.

🎶

Music

Live dhol, tabla, flute and harmonium, with soul-stirring bhajans and chaupais that carry every scene.
