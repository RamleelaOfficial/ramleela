Ten thousand learn. Five thousand perform.

10,000+ is our enrolment goal. 5,000+ is a separate target: the students who travel to Mumbai and take part. Selection is fair and published in advance, so participation is earned, never assumed from enrolment alone.

Wankhede action plan

A 5,000-participant event is a major live-production and public-safety project. Begin with the venue owner and qualified professionals, and never assume access, capacity, technical specifications or security arrangements.

Please do not publish invented stadium specifications, security procedures, turf-loading figures, VIP access claims or guaranteed guest attendance. The venue and competent authorities must verify and approve every technical and safety decision.

Franchise network calculator

Model how branches, students and fees add up to the 10,000 target, and what each certified teacher earns.

This is a scenario tool, not a revenue forecast. It subtracts branch costs from gross fees and splits the rest. It leaves out taxes, central overhead, marketing, payment fees, refunds and scholarships. Use real accounts, and prove each branch’s economics before opening the next.
